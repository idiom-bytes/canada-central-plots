# Tahltan Territory Mineral Stakeholders — Research Notes

Scope: Tahltan Nation territory, northwestern BC ("Golden Triangle" mineral belt).
Covers mining companies/tenure holders, ownership (incl. foreign & notable
individual investors), NGO/advocacy landscape, and lobbyist-registry findings.

Sourcing key:
- **VERIFIED** — primary source (company filing, government release, official
  registry record) directly confirms the claim.
- **REPORTED** — credible secondary source (established news outlet, trade
  press) states the claim; not independently confirmed against a primary doc.
- **UNVERIFIED / COULD NOT CONFIRM** — claim searched for and not found, or
  found only in low-confidence sources; flagged as a research gap rather than
  asserted.

Research date: 2026-08-12. Ownership/shareholder percentages are current as of
search date and will drift — treat as a snapshot, not a standing fact.

## 1. Mining companies / mineral tenure holders in Tahltan territory

### 1.1 Red Chris Mine

- **Operator/majority owner:** Newmont Corporation — 70%. **VERIFIED** (Newmont
  operations page). Newmont is incorporated in Delaware, headquartered in
  Denver, Colorado, USA; dual-listed NYSE: NEM / TSX: NGT.
  https://operations.newmont.com/canada/red-chris-canada/
- **Minority owner:** Imperial Metals Corporation — 30%. **VERIFIED**.
  https://imperialmetals.com/our-operations/red-chris-mine
- **Ownership chain:** Imperial Metals sold a 70% interest to Newcrest Red
  Chris Mining Ltd in August 2019; Newcrest (then Australian, ASX-listed) was
  acquired by Newmont in November 2023, transferring operatorship to Newmont.
  **REPORTED** (Canadian Mining Journal / Global News history piece).
  https://globalnews.ca/news/11303093/red-chris-mine-history/
- **Tahltan agreement:** Operates under an impact benefit and co-management
  agreement with the Tahltan Nation; the 2026 underground expansion approval
  came through a **consent-based process** under the province's 2023
  Section 7 agreement with the Tahltan Central Government (TCG), including an
  amended Environmental Assessment Certificate. TCG formally consented to the
  expansion (dated May 22, per trade press). **VERIFIED/REPORTED** (BC
  government-adjacent coverage; TCG's own site corroborates the 2023
  Section 7 framework — see §1.6).
  https://www.canadianminingjournal.com/news/newmonts-red-chris-underground-expansion-gets-regulatory-green-light-in-bc/
  https://www.mining.com/newmonts-red-chris-underground-expansion-gets-regulatory-green-light-in-bc/
- **Federal fast-track status:** Red Chris expansion is listed on Canada's
  Major Projects Office national projects list. **VERIFIED** (canada.ca).
  https://www.canada.ca/en/privy-council/major-projects-office/projects/national/red-chris.html
- **Note on Imperial Metals' broader environmental record:** Imperial Metals
  operated the Mount Polley mine (Secwépemc/Xatśūll territory, central BC —
  **not** Tahltan territory) where a 2014 tailings dam failure occurred. This
  is included only as company-level risk context; it did not occur in Tahltan
  territory and should not be presented as a Tahltan-territory incident.
  **REPORTED** (widely covered; not separately re-verified here).

### 1.2 Galore Creek

- **Ownership:** Galore Creek Mining Corporation (GCMC) is a 50:50 joint
  venture between Teck Resources Limited and Newmont Corporation. **VERIFIED**
  (Teck project page, GCMC site).
  https://www.teck.com/operations/canada/projects/galore-creek-project/
  https://www.gcmc.ca/galore-creek-project/
- **How Newmont entered:** Newmont acquired a 50% stake in the Galore Creek
  partnership for US$275 million (from NovaGold, the prior JV partner).
  **REPORTED**.
  https://miningdigital.com/automation-and-ai/newmont-mining-acquires-50-stake-galore-creek-partnership-dollar275-million
- **Tahltan agreement:** A Participation Agreement was signed in 2006,
  covering technical/regulatory engagement, student bursaries, and
  employment/business opportunities. **VERIFIED** (GCMC site).
- **Recent development:** BC and the Tahltan Central Government entered a
  decision-making/review agreement on Galore Creek in November 2023, for
  collaborative assessment of proposed project changes. **REPORTED**
  (Construct Connect / BC-linked trade press).
  https://canada.constructconnect.com/joc/news/government/2023/11/b-c-and-tahltan-nation-enter-agreement-on-galore-creek-review
- **Foreign ownership note:** Teck is Canadian (Vancouver-HQ, TSX/NYSE);
  Newmont is US-domiciled — so Galore Creek is a 50% US-domiciled / 50%
  Canadian-domiciled JV at the corporate level. Ordinary institutional
  ownership within each company is discussed in §2.

### 1.3 Schaft Creek

- **Ownership:** Schaft Creek Joint Venture — Teck Resources 75% (operator),

  https://copperfoxmetals.com/projects/schaft-creek-project/overview/
- **Copper Fox Metals corporate profile:** Calgary-headquartered, TSX
  Venture-listed (CUU.V); largest shareholder is reported as Ernesto

- **Tahltan engagement:** Teck and the Tahltan Nation Development Corporation
  (TNDC) partnered on a solar power project at the Schaft Creek site, plus
  educational/youth/cultural support. **VERIFIED** (Teck news release).
  https://www.teck.com/news/stories/2025/schaft-creek-solar-facility
  No dedicated Impact Benefit Agreement specific to Schaft Creek was found
  (project remains at exploration/PEA stage, not permitted for construction)
  — **COULD NOT CONFIRM** a signed IBA; flagged as gap.

### 1.4 Brucejack

- **Ownership chain:** Pretium Resources (built and operated Brucejack) →
  acquired by Newcrest Mining (Australian, ASX-listed) in March 2022 →
  Newcrest acquired by Newmont in November 2023. Brucejack is now
  wholly Newmont-operated. **VERIFIED/REPORTED** (Newmont operations
  filings; Mining.com/Wikipedia history).
  https://operations.newmont.com/_doc/operation/bluejack/annual-reports/Brucejack%20Mine%202024%20CEAA%20Annual%20Report.pdf
- **Territorial overlap note:** Brucejack sits within territory asserted by
  **both** the Tsetsaut Skii km Lax Ha (TSKLH) Nation and the Tahltan Nation,
  and near the Nisga'a Nation's treaty lands. This is a contested/overlapping
  claims area — do not present Brucejack as exclusively Tahltan territory.
  **VERIFIED** (Newmont's own CEAA annual report states this explicitly).
- **Revenue sharing:** BC signed an agreement with the Tahltan Central
  Government to share mineral tax revenue from the Brucejack gold mine —
  reported at roughly $7 million/year to TCG. **REPORTED** (BC government news
  release referenced via search; original 2018 announcement).
  https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2018IRR0034-001215

### 1.5 Eskay Creek

- **Ownership:** Skeena Resources Limited (now operating/branding as "Skeena
  Gold & Silver") owns 100% of the Eskay Creek Gold-Silver Project.
  **VERIFIED** (company press releases).
- **Tahltan agreement:** Tahltan Nation voted in December 2025 to support an
  Impact Benefit Agreement for Eskay Creek, covering employment, business
  opportunities, training, an elders' facility, and "meaningful financial
  participation." **VERIFIED** (Skeena/GlobeNewswire release).
  https://skeenagoldsilver.com/news-release/skeena-announces-successful-tahltan-nation-vote-supporting-the-eskay-creek-impact-benefit-agreement/
- **Environmental Assessment Certificate** issued January 27, 2026, plus
  federal Impact Assessment Act approval. **VERIFIED** (GlobeNewswire /
  BC government release).
  https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/27/3226273/0/en/Skeena-Gold-Silver-Secures-Environmental-Assessment-Certificate-and-Federal-Impact-Assessment-Approval-for-Eskay-Creek.html
  https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026MCM0008-000078
- **"First Declaration Act Section 7 agreement" claim:** BC's own release
  characterizes Eskay Creek as BC's first project approved through a Section 7
  agreement under the Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples Act. Note
  the Red Chris consent-based agreement (2023) predates this in some framing —
  treat "first" claims from company/government press releases cautiously and
  verify the specific legal mechanism (Section 7 order vs. earlier consent
  frameworks) before republishing the "historic first" framing.
- **Foreign ownership:** Skeena Gold & Silver is Canadian (Vancouver HQ,
  TSX/NYSE listed as SKE). Institutional shareholders include BlackRock
  (~1.6% per a March 2025 13G-type filing, **REPORTED**, gurufocus) and UBS.
  A specific Anglo American strategic stake was **searched for and not
  confirmed — UNVERIFIED / COULD NOT CONFIRM**; Anglo American appears
  elsewhere in BC mining-lobbying data (see §5) but not tied to Skeena
  ownership in sources found.
- **Mineral tax revenue sharing:** TCG and the Province are entering a
  mineral-tax revenue-sharing agreement tied to Eskay Creek, with first
  payment potentially as early as 2027–28. **REPORTED**.

### 1.6 KSM (Kerr-Sulphurets-Mitchell)

- **Ownership:** 100% Seabridge Gold Inc. (Toronto-HQ, TSX: SEA / NYSE: SA).
  **VERIFIED** (company statements).
- **Tahltan agreement:** Tahltan Nation voted 77.8% in favour of a KSM Impact
  Benefits Agreement / Co-operation and Benefits Agreement with Seabridge.
  **VERIFIED** (Seabridge press release via Newsfile).
  https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/45434/Tahltan-Nation-and-Seabridge-Gold-Execute-FarReaching-Agreement-Covering-Protections-of-Indigenous-Rights-and-the-Environment-and-Shared-Economic-Benefits-for-KSM-Project
- Seabridge also holds a benefits agreement with the Nisga'a Nation and states
  it has directed ~CA$400 million to Indigenous-affiliated business contracts
  (company-reported figure; **REPORTED**, not independently audited).
- **Provincial priority-project status:** BC named KSM a "priority project"
  (April 2026) for expedited permitting coordination. **REPORTED**
  (Streetwise Reports / Seabridge press release).
- **Litigation — important, keep the parties distinct:** The **Tsetsaut Skii
  km Lax Ha (TSKLH) Nation** — a distinct Nation from the Tahltan, with
  overlapping/contested asserted territory in the Treaty Creek Valley/KSM
  area — won a judicial review at the BC Supreme Court (decision reported
  June 2026, Justice Emily Burke) finding the Province breached its duty to
  consult TSKLH before granting KSM's "Substantially Started" designation.
  The Court quashed the SSD and ordered renewed consultation with a 90-day
  window for TSKLH submissions. **VERIFIED/REPORTED** (CBC News, National
  Observer, Newswire legal-notice release; specific CanLII citation not
  located in this pass — flagged as a gap for someone with CanLII access).
  https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/british-columbia/province-consult-duty-gold-mine-9.7229420
  https://www.nationalobserver.com/2026/06/09/news/bc-court-finds-province-didnt-properly-consult-first-nation-northwest-gold-mine
  https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/legal-win-for-tsetsaut-skii-km-lax-ha-nation-b-c-supreme-court-strikes-down-substantial-start-determination-for-ksm-mine-and-orders-proper-consultation-quashing-led-to-justice-not-ruling-on-ssd-reasonableness-846724457.html
  **This is TSKLH litigation, not Tahltan litigation** — do not attribute the
  court case to the Tahltan Nation. It is relevant to the dashboard only as a
  process/oversight item tied to the same KSM project.
- **Foreign ownership:** Seabridge Gold — institutional ownership ~38–44%,
  largest single institutional holder reported as Kopernik Global Investors
  (~7.99%, US-based). Other holders: Van Eck, Vanguard, CIBC World Markets,
  Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan, Citadel. **REPORTED** (aggregator sites —
  WallStreetZen/Yahoo Finance/Fintel; cross-check against SEDI/SEC 13F before
  using precise percentages in the dashboard).

## 3. Foreign ownership vs. "foreign interference" — explicit distinction

- Every major operator above (Newmont [US], Teck [Canada], Seabridge Gold
  [Canada], Skeena Gold & Silver [Canada], Copper Fox Metals [Canada],
  Imperial Metals [Canada]) is a normal, legally-domiciled, publicly traded
  (or Canadian-controlled in Copper Fox's case) mining company. Newmont's
  US domicile and its predecessor Newcrest's former Australian domicile are
  **ordinary foreign direct investment**, fully disclosed in public filings.
  This should be reported factually as foreign ownership/investment, not as
  "interference."
- A general search for CSIS material on foreign interference in Canadian
  critical-minerals/mining turned up results from the **US think tank CSIS
  (Center for Strategic and International Studies)**, not Canada's Canadian
  Security Intelligence Service. Its analyses discuss a global pattern of
  Chinese state-owned enterprises acquiring stakes in *other* critical-mineral
  projects (e.g., federally ordered divestment of Chinese SOE minority stakes
  in some Canadian lithium companies, unrelated to Tahltan territory) — **not**
  Tahltan territory or any of the six projects above. **No source was found
  alleging foreign government interference specific to Tahltan territory or
  any Golden Triangle project.** Per the investigation's own quality bar, this
  means the "foreign involvement" risk category for this investigation should
  currently read as **ordinary foreign investment only** — no interference
  finding, no interference framing, until/unless a specific sourced claim
  surfaces.

## 4. NGO / advocacy landscape

- **SkeenaWild Conservation Trust** — mining-impacts-focused conservation
  NGO. Published a report "Key Risks & Lessons at the Red Chris Mine" (~March
  17, 2025) raising tailings/operational concerns. The **Tahltan Central
  Government published a public open letter response** (April 4, 2025) that
  did not directly rebut SkeenaWild's technical concerns but emphasized TCG's
  own governance/co-management role and ongoing remediation work — evidence
  that TCG's official position and SkeenaWild's advocacy position are not
  identical, without characterizing them as adversarial. **VERIFIED**
  (TCG's own site hosts the open letter).
  https://tahltan.org/open-letter-response-to-skeenawilds-red-chris-report/
  SkeenaWild also participated in the Community Advisory Committee for the
  Eskay Creek Revitalization environmental assessment, alongside Salmon
  Beyond Borders and the Northern Confluence Initiative. **REPORTED**.
- **Rivers Without Borders** — US/Alaska-linked transboundary-rivers advocacy
  organization; documents mining proposals in Tahltan/Stikine watershed
  territory (including historical opposition to Fortune Minerals' proposed
  Mount Klappan coal project). Works alongside SkeenaWild.
  https://www.riverswithoutborders.org/iskut-stikine
- **Salmon Beyond Borders** — transboundary salmon-watershed advocacy group,
  active in Eskay Creek assessment process (see above).
- **Klabona Keepers** — grassroots movement of Tahltan Elders/community
  members (matriarchs Bertha Louie; activists Rhoda Quock, John Nole cited),
  distinct from and operating alongside (not identical to) the elected Tahltan
  Central Government. Documented campaigns: blockades/resistance that
  contributed to Shell Canada abandoning a coalbed-methane project (2004,
  ~4,000 km² of Tahltan land, formally dropped 2012) and to the defeat of
  Fortune Minerals' proposed open-pit coal mine on Mount Klappan after ~15
  years of opposition. As of the September 2022 Narwhal article, they were
  monitoring Newmont's mineral-claim purchases near Iskut village.
  **REPORTED** (The Narwhal, a nonprofit investigative outlet — treat as
  credible secondary source; primary blockade-era sourcing not
  independently re-verified here).
  https://thenarwhal.ca/klabona-keepers-tahltan-nation/
- **David Suzuki Foundation** — has covered the Tahltan's "Sacred Headwaters"
  campaign (Mount Klappan/Klappan area) historically. **REPORTED**
  (organization's own site, general framing, not a funding disclosure).
  https://davidsuzuki.org/story/tahltans-decades-long-struggle-to-protect-sacred-headwaters/
- **Association for Mineral Exploration BC (AME)** — industry association;
  **registered BC lobbyist** on Tahltan-specific matters (see §5) — this is
  the clearest documented industry-advocacy presence specific to Tahltan
  territory found in this research pass.
- **Mining Association of BC** — publishes general reconciliation-framed
  material referencing Tahltan agreements as a model, but no specific
  lobbying-registry record naming Tahltan was found for this association
  (distinct from AME) — **COULD NOT CONFIRM** a direct registered-lobbying
  link; only public advocacy content was found.
  https://mining.bc.ca/reconciliation/
- **Funding disclosure gap:** None of the environmental NGOs above (SkeenaWild,
  Rivers Without Borders, Salmon Beyond Borders) had itemized funding sources
  located in this pass (would require pulling CRA charity filings /
  T3010 returns for the Canadian-registered ones, or US 990s for
  Rivers Without Borders if US-registered). **Flagged as a research gap** —
  recommend a follow-up pass pulling CRA's charity listings
  (https://apps.cra-arc.gc.ca/ebci/hacc/srch/) by legal name for each
  Canadian-registered NGO before publishing specific funder claims.
