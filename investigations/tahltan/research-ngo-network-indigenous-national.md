# Tahltan Territory: NGO/Advocacy Network Deep Dive — Founding, Funding, Governance, Cross-Connections

Companion deep-dive to `research-stakeholders.md` §4 (NGO/advocacy landscape), which already
surface-identified these three organizations. This file does not repeat that section's basic
identification — it adds founding history, funding/governance structure, and an explicit
cross-connection check against the mining companies, individuals, and officials already mapped
in `research-stakeholders.md` and `research-officials-legal.md`.

Scope: **Klabona Keepers**, **David Suzuki Foundation**, **MiningWatch Canada**.

Sourcing key (same as companion files):
- **VERIFIED** — primary source (org's own site, CRA registry, court record, government filing)
  directly confirms the claim.
- **REPORTED** — credible secondary source (established news outlet, nonprofit-sector database)
  states the claim; not independently confirmed against a primary document.
- **UNVERIFIED / COULD NOT CONFIRM** — claim searched for and not found, or found only in a
  low-confidence/unsourced summary; flagged as a research gap rather than asserted.

Research date: 2026-08-12.

A note on framing, per the task brief: Klabona Keepers represents Tahltan community members
exercising their own advocacy on their own territory. Its documented history and activities are
reported factually below, without characterizing its legitimacy relative to the elected Tahltan
Central Government (TCG) — the two are described only in terms of what the record actually shows.

## 1. Klabona Keepers

### 1.1 Founding — triggering event and founding members

- **Formation:** A grassroots blockade movement of Tahltan families and Elders that formed in
  response to two industrial threats to Tl'abāne (the "Sacred Headwaters" of the Stikine, Nass,
  and Skeena Rivers, near Iskut, BC): **Royal Dutch Shell's plan to drill 1,000+ coalbed methane
  wells** across roughly 4,000 km² of Tahltan territory, and **Fortune Minerals' proposed
  40,000-hectare open-pit coal mine on Mount Klappan**. **REPORTED** (Briarpatch Magazine, The
  Narwhal).
  https://briarpatchmagazine.com/articles/view/to-take-the-land-away-from-the-children
  https://thenarwhal.ca/klabona-keepers-tahltan-nation/
- **Sequence:** The first blockade went up against Fortune Minerals in **2005**; the families and
  Elders who organized it became known as the Klabona Keepers after blockading Shell's coalbed
  methane operations in **2006**. A **February 25, 2005** "Tahltan Elders Statement" — *Dena nenn
  Sogga neh 'ine* ("Protectors and Keepers of the Land") — declared a moratorium on resource
  development in Tahltan territory and is the earliest dated primary document found for this
  movement; MiningWatch Canada's website hosted/republished it the same year (see §3.4 for the
  cross-connection). **VERIFIED** (document itself, hosted at miningwatch.ca; independently
  reported founding sequence via Briarpatch/Narwhal).
  https://miningwatch.ca/blog/2005/2/25/tahltan-elders-statement-dena-nenn-sogga-neh-ine-protectors-and-keepers-land
- **Named founding/early members:** **Bertha Louie** (matriarch), **Rhoda Quock** (spokesperson,
  later a producer of the 2022 documentary), **John Nole** (Iskut Band councillor at the time).
  **Robert Jakesta** (Quock's son) is documented as having grown up participating in the
  blockades. **REPORTED** (The Narwhal).
- **2006 Iskut blockade detail (new finding, not in the companion stakeholders file):** On
  September 9, 2006, **Lillian Moyer**, a 67-year-old Tahltan Elder from Telegraph Creek, was
  arrested at a two-month-long blockade near Iskut opposing **bcMetals Corporation's** copper-gold
  exploration on the Todagin Plateau (a traditional-use area). Over 100 supporters attended,
  including Gitxsan and Wet'suwet'en representatives. Rhoda Quock is quoted criticizing a
  concurrent oil spill into Coyote Creek during the blockade. **VERIFIED** (MiningWatch Canada's
  own contemporaneous news post).
  https://miningwatch.ca/news/2006/9/11/grandmother-arrested-iskut-blockade

### 1.2 2014 Red Chris blockade — new finding directly relevant to this investigation's flagship mine

- On **August 8, 2014**, three days after Imperial Metals' **Mount Polley tailings dam failure**
  (a different mine, different territory — see the caution already logged in
  `research-stakeholders.md` §1.1), the **Klabona Keepers blockaded Imperial Metals' Red Chris
  Mine** near Iskut — the same Red Chris Mine that is now Newmont/Imperial Metals' flagship
  Tahltan-territory operation in this investigation — blocking employee, fuel, and shuttle
  traffic and demanding operations stop pending a safety review. Then-TCG President **Chad Norman
  Day** (a named signatory throughout `research-officials-legal.md`) acknowledged the action while
  expressing general support for environmental protection; Imperial Metals president **Brian
  Kynoch** apologized publicly for Mount Polley the same week. **REPORTED** (Intercontinental Cry
  / IC Magazine, contemporaneous).
  https://icmagazine.org/tahltan-elders-blockade-red-chris-mine-mount-polley-breach-25256/
- This is a documented instance of Klabona Keepers directly targeting one of the six mines already
  mapped in `research-stakeholders.md` (Red Chris), not previously logged in that file, which
  recorded only the Shell coalbed methane and Fortune Minerals/Mount Klappan campaigns.

### 1.3 Formal status — incorporation, fiscal sponsorship

- **No evidence found that Klabona Keepers is formally incorporated** as a society, non-profit
  corporation, or registered charity under any federal or BC registry searched. Every source
  describes it as "a small group of Tahltan people," "an organization of Tahltan Elders and
  families," or similar informal-collective language — never as a registered legal entity.
  **UNVERIFIED / COULD NOT CONFIRM incorporation; absence of evidence, not confirmation of
  informality by exclusion** — a direct BC Registry Services / Corporations Canada name search
  was not run in this pass and would close this gap definitively.
- **Fiscal sponsorship:** For tax-deductible donations (particularly tied to *The Klabona Keepers*
  documentary and its associated youth land-based education program, launched fall 2017), the
  group has operated through fiscal-sponsorship/convening arrangements with **MakeWay** (the
  Canadian charitable platform that succeeded Tides Canada — itself a well-known fiscal sponsor
  for dozens of Canadian environmental and Indigenous-led groups) and **SWCC (Skeena Watershed
  Conservation Coalition)**, which "can offer charitable receipt for larger donations" on the
  Keepers' behalf. **REPORTED** (Klabona Keepers' own film-project site).
  http://beyondboardingcollective.com/donatekk
- **Documented funding to date** (tied specifically to the film/education-program fundraising
  push, not a general operating budget — no annual financial statement was located): a 2021 Good
  Pitch/Story Money Impact fundraising event recorded pledges including **$20,000 from the
  Inspirit Foundation**, **$60,000 via the Skeena Watershed Coalition** (multiple donors pooled),
  **$12,500 from Patagonia**, and **$15,000 from LUSH Cosmetics**, plus numerous smaller individual
  donations. **REPORTED** (Story Money Impact, the film's impact-producing partner).
  https://www.storymoneyimpact.com/klabona-keepers-report

### 1.4 Current organizers / spokespeople

- **Rhoda Quock** remains the most consistently identified spokesperson across sources spanning
  2006–2022 (interviewed directly in The Narwhal's September 2022 piece, sometimes quoted through
  her husband Peter Jakesta in other coverage). **John Nole** continued advocacy work per the same
  2022 reporting. **REPORTED**.
- **Chief Marie Quock**, Chief of the Iskut Band Council, is named in Klabona
  Keepers-adjacent fundraising/film materials and is separately documented in
  `research-officials-legal.md` §3 as a named signatory (alongside Chief Carmen McPhee of the
  Tahltan Band Council) to the **2020 Shared Prosperity Agreement** between BC and the Tahltan —
  i.e., an elected official under the TCG structure. She shares a surname with founding/early
  Klabona Keepers figures Rhoda Quock and (per the 2022 documentary's credits) Mary Quock. Quock is
  a common Tahltan/Iskut family name and **this is not evidence of a formal organizational tie or
  that these are the same family branch** — flagged here only because it is the clearest
  documented point of person-level proximity found between the grassroots movement and TCG-linked
  elected governance in a community as small as Iskut (population in the low hundreds).
  **UNVERIFIED / COULD NOT CONFIRM** as a direct familial or organizational link; reported as an
  observed naming coincidence worth noting, not a confirmed connection.

### 1.5 Relationship to the elected Tahltan Central Government (TCG)

- No source reviewed in this pass documents an explicit rupture, formal dispute, or public
  conflict between Klabona Keepers and TCG. Coverage instead shows **parallel, sometimes
  overlapping activity**: TCG's elected leadership (Chad Norman Day in 2014 and again as signatory
  to the 2017 Klappan protective agreement covering 286,000 hectares) has at points publicly
  acknowledged or aligned with outcomes the Klabona Keepers campaigned for, without the two bodies
  being presented as the same entity or formally merged. **REPORTED**.
- As of the **most recent substantive third-party coverage located** — The Narwhal's September 30,
  2022 feature — Klabona Keepers were reported to be **monitoring Newmont's mineral-claim
  purchases near Iskut village** (already logged in `research-stakeholders.md` §4). **No dated,
  substantive coverage from 2023–2026 was found** in this pass (a Narwhal on-site search for
  "Klabona" returned only the 2022 piece; general web search for 2024–2026 Klabona Keepers activity
  did not surface a specific update). This is reported as a **research gap** — it should not be
  read as evidence the group is inactive, only that no updated public reporting was located.
  https://thenarwhal.ca/klabona-keepers-tahltan-nation/

## 3. MiningWatch Canada

### 3.1 Founding history

- MiningWatch Canada launched **April 1, 1999**, as a pan-Canadian coalition initiative, growing
  out of discussions among environmental, social-justice, Indigenous, and labour organizations
  concerned about mining's effects on the environment and on the safety/livelihoods of communities
  in mining-affected areas. **REPORTED** (MiningWatch's own 25th-anniversary campaign materials;
  ESCR-Net; Influence Watch).
  https://www.miningwatch.ca/blog/2024/4/24/25-years-building-mining-justice
- **Founder:** **Joan Kuyek** is credited as founder, with the stated intent of creating a
  platform where "academics, journalists, and popular publishers" could scrutinize mining
  development. **REPORTED** (Influence Watch).
  https://www.influencewatch.org/organization/miningwatch-canada/
- It was formed as a **coalition of member organizations** rather than a standalone charity from
  the outset (see §3.5 for the current member list, which includes a direct Tahltan-relevant
  cross-connection).

### 3.2 Legal/charitable structure — deliberately not a registered charity

- MiningWatch Canada itself is **not a CRA-registered charity** — sources describe it as a
  federally registered non-profit organization/society that deliberately avoided charitable
  registration because charitable status historically constrained the scope of political/advocacy
  activity a Canadian charity could undertake. As a result, **MiningWatch Canada cannot issue tax
  receipts directly.** **REPORTED**.
- To allow tax-deductible giving, MiningWatch operates alongside a **sister registered charity**,
  the **Canary Research Institute for Mining, Environment, and Health**, incorporated **November
  3, 2003**, whose stated charitable purpose is education and poverty reduction relating to
  mineral development's impacts on human and environmental health. Its **CRA charitable
  registration number is 871039400RR0001**. **VERIFIED** (CanadaHelps charity profile page).
  https://www.canadahelps.org/en/charities/canary-research-institute-for-mining-environment-and-health/
  https://canaryinstitute.ca/
- No itemized T3010 revenue breakdown for the Canary Research Institute specifically was pulled in
  this pass — **flagged as a gap** if precise Canary-Institute-only financials are needed
  (distinct from the MiningWatch-branded operating figures in §3.4 below, which come from
  MiningWatch's own annual reporting, not necessarily routed entirely through Canary).

## 4. Cross-connection matrix — summary

This is the most important section per the task brief. Findings, stated plainly:

1. **MiningWatch Canada ↔ Rivers Without Borders — CONFIRMED institutional link.** Rivers Without
   Borders (documented Tahltan-territory advocate in `research-stakeholders.md` §4) is a formal
   dues/coalition member of MiningWatch Canada. See §3.5.
2. **MiningWatch Canada ↔ Klabona Keepers/Tahltan resistance movement — CONFIRMED historical
   advocacy link, pre-dating the 2010 SCC litigation.** MiningWatch directly published the 2005
   Tahltan Elders moratorium statement and covered the 2006 Iskut blockade in which Rhoda Quock
   (later a named Klabona Keepers spokesperson and film producer) is directly quoted. See §3.6.
3. **Klabona Keepers ↔ Skeena Watershed Conservation Coalition (SWCC) — CONFIRMED funding/
   fiscal-sponsorship link.** SWCC (founded 2004, works on Sacred Headwaters protection alongside
   Klabona Keepers) is a documented fiscal-sponsorship/fundraising partner for Klabona Keepers'
   film and education-program donations, alongside MakeWay. SWCC is a distinct organization from
   SkeenaWild Conservation Trust (already in the companion file) but operates in the same
   Skeena-watershed advocacy space and shares MakeWay as a charitable-platform relationship — the
   task brief's supplementary-check instruction is satisfied here: SWCC/MakeWay is the connective
   tissue between Klabona Keepers and the broader Skeena-watershed NGO cluster (SkeenaWild, Salmon
   Beyond Borders, Northern Confluence Initiative) referenced in the brief, though a **direct
   person-level or funding link specifically to SkeenaWild, Salmon Beyond Borders, or Northern
   Confluence Initiative was not confirmed** in this pass beyond the shared watershed-advocacy
