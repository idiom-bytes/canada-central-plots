# Tahltan-Adjacent Conservation NGO Network — Deep Dive

Scope: A deeper pass on the four environmental/conservation advocacy organizations
identified at a surface level in `research-stakeholders.md` §4 (SkeenaWild Conservation
Trust, Salmon Beyond Borders, Rivers Without Borders, Northern Confluence Initiative).
This file does not repeat their basic advocacy positions — see `research-stakeholders.md`
§4 for that — and instead covers: founding history, legal/fiscal structure, funding,
boards/staff, and — the central task — whether there is any documented overlap in
people or funding (a) between these four organizations, or (b) between any of them and
the mining companies, investors, or officials already mapped in
`research-stakeholders.md` and `research-officials-legal.md`.

Sourcing key (same convention as the other Tahltan research files):
- **VERIFIED** — primary source (the organization's own site/filing/annual report, a
  government or funder registry) directly confirms the claim.
- **REPORTED** — credible secondary source states the claim; not independently
  cross-checked against a primary document.
- **UNVERIFIED / COULD NOT CONFIRM** — searched for and not found, or found only in a
  low-confidence source; flagged as a research gap rather than asserted.

Research date: 2026-08-12. This pass hit the session's web-search tool budget before a
handful of secondary checks (individual trustee bios) could be completed — those gaps
are marked explicitly in §6 rather than filled with inference.

## 2. Salmon Beyond Borders

### 2.1 Legal structure — not a standalone entity

Salmon Beyond Borders is **not independently incorporated**. It is a campaign of
**SalmonState**, which is itself **fiscally sponsored by the New Venture Fund** (a US
501(c)(3) fiscal-sponsorship vehicle managed by Arabella Advisors). **VERIFIED**,
cross-confirmed across three independent sources: New Venture Fund's own blog citing
Salmon Beyond Borders as a case study, SalmonState's own "About" page, and
InfluenceWatch.org's organizational profile.
https://newventurefund.org/2021/07/14/how-to-forge-local-relationships-and-achieve-state-wide-success/
https://salmonstate.org/about-salmonstate
https://www.influencewatch.org/organization/salmonstate/

So the structure is: **Salmon Beyond Borders (campaign) → SalmonState (parent
campaign/brand) → New Venture Fund (501(c)(3) fiscal sponsor, legal entity).** Donations
and grants flow to New Venture Fund on Salmon Beyond Borders'/SalmonState's behalf; there
is no separate EIN or Form 990 filed specifically for Salmon Beyond Borders.

### 2.2 Founding and leadership

  campaign as having "emerged following the Mount Polley dam failure" (August 2014) —

  August 2014 triggering event); treat the exact founding month as approximate.

  https://salmonstate.org/salmon-stories/jill-weitz

  Enforcement Officer for the **Alaska Department of Environmental Conservation**

## 3. Rivers Without Borders

### 3.1 Founding and legal structure

- **Origin:** Grew out of 1990s transboundary conservation advocacy in the region
  (secondary sources tie this to opposition to a copper-mine proposal in the
  Alsek-Tatshenshini watershed in the early-to-mid 1990s). Formally organized in **1999**
  as the **Transboundary Watershed Alliance**, a coalition of 22 founding
  Canadian/US member groups. Became a **distinct organization renamed Rivers Without
  Borders in 2007**. **VERIFIED/REPORTED** (Wikipedia, cross-confirmed by the org's own
  "About" page and by Tides.org's project listing).
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rivers_Without_Borders
  https://www.riverswithoutborders.org/about-rivers-without-borders
  https://www.tides.org/project/fiscally-sponsored-project/rivers-without-borders/
- **Legal structure:** Fiscally sponsored **project of Tides Center**, a US 501(c)(3),
  for its US operations, based in Port Townsend, WA. **VERIFIED** (org's own site + Tides
  Center's own project page). Because it is fiscally sponsored rather than independently
  incorporated, Rivers Without Borders **does not file its own separate IRS Form 990** —
  its finances are consolidated within Tides Center's filing, which means grant-level
  detail specific to Rivers Without Borders is **not retrievable via a standalone
  ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer / Form 990 lookup**, contrary to the original research
  brief's assumption. This is flagged explicitly as a structural reason the "pull its
  Form 990" research angle does not directly apply — see §6.
- **A specific false positive to correct:** An early search hit for "Rivers Without
  Borders" on ProPublica's Nonprofit Explorer actually resolved to an unrelated
  California entity, **"Borders Without Boundaries Inc"** (EIN 84-3119304), whose
  officers are **Thomas J. Barrack Jr.** and "TJ Barrack" (a name-order/keyword
  coincidence, not the same organization — Borders Without Boundaries reports ~$550/year
  in revenue and has no connection to transboundary BC/Alaska conservation). Noting this
  explicitly so it is not mistakenly cited as a Rivers Without Borders funding record in
  future work.
  https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/843119304
- **Canadian-side operations:** Historical secondary sourcing (Wikipedia) describes
  Rivers Without Borders' Canada work as run "as a project of Tides Canada Initiatives."
  The organization's own current "About" page, fetched directly in this pass, mentions
  only the US Tides Center fiscal sponsorship and does not itself confirm a current
  MakeWay (Tides Canada's successor brand, see §4) relationship — **this could not be
  independently re-confirmed as current in this pass** and is flagged as a specific gap
  in §6, because if still accurate it would be a second documented fiscal-sponsor-level
  connection point to Northern Confluence (also hosted by MakeWay, see §4).

## 5. Cross-organization overlap analysis (the central task)

### 5.1 The one clear, documented network-level connection

**Northern Confluence Initiative's director, Nikki Skuce, co-chairs the BC Mining Law
Reform network (BCMLR) together with Jamie Kneen of MiningWatch Canada.** MiningWatch
Canada is **the same organization that was the plaintiff in MiningWatch Canada v.
Canada (Fisheries and Oceans), 2010 SCC 2** — the Supreme Court of Canada case, already
documented in `research-officials-legal.md` §6.1, specifically concerning the federal
environmental-assessment scoping for the **Red Chris Mine** (Newmont/Imperial Metals).
MiningWatch Canada is also independently listed as a formal Northern Confluence partner
organization (§4.3). This connects Northern Confluence, through its director's
co-chairship of a joint advocacy body, to the organization behind the only litigation in
the entire Tahltan-territory research set that predates and specifically targets Red
Chris's original federal approval.

This is reported factually: a documented professional/advocacy-network tie (shared
co-chairship of a named coalition body, and a formal partner-organization listing), not
a financial or governance relationship (no shared board seats, no funding flow
identified between Northern Confluence and MiningWatch Canada in this pass).

### 5.2 Direct partner-list overlap among three of the four target organizations

SkeenaWild, Salmon Beyond Borders, and Northern Confluence all appear together on
Northern Confluence's own published partners list (§4.3), and all three were already
noted in `research-stakeholders.md` §4 as co-participants in the Eskay Creek
Revitalization environmental assessment's Community Advisory Committee. This is a
**genuine, documented, multi-source-confirmed network relationship** among these three
organizations. Rivers Without Borders sits adjacent to this network (shared subject
matter, "works alongside" language in prior research) but was not found in this pass to
be formally partnered with Northern Confluence in the same documented way.

### 5.3 Funder-network adjacency (not governance)

Wild Salmon Center is documented in this pass as both a funder of SalmonState/Salmon
Beyond Borders ($100,000, 2023, per InfluenceWatch/990-derived data) and a partner
organization of SkeenaWild (per Wild Salmon Center's own partnerships page). This is a
funding/convening-network adjacency between two of the four target organizations — not a
shared board seat or shared staff member, and not evidence of common control. Reported
factually as a network-proximity finding.

### 5.4 No shared personnel found between the four target organizations themselves

Despite the network-level connections in §5.1–5.3, **no individual person was found in
this pass sitting on the board, staff, or steering committee of more than one of the
four target organizations** (SkeenaWild, Salmon Beyond Borders, Rivers Without Borders,
Northern Confluence). Each organization's leadership roster, cross-checked against the
other three, produced no name matches. This is an explicit negative finding, not an
assumption — it should be read as "no overlap found in this pass," with the caveat that
three SkeenaWild trustee bios (Collingwood, Wroblewski, Hill) could not be fully
researched due to the session's search-tool budget being exhausted (§6), so this is not
a fully exhaustive board-level check.

### 5.5 No overlap found with the mapped mining companies, investors, or officials

Across all four organizations' boards, staff, and steering committees identified in this
pass, **no individual was found with a documented financial stake, employment history,
board seat, or family connection to any of**: Newmont, Imperial Metals, Teck, Seabridge
Gold, Skeena Gold & Silver, Copper Fox Metals, Kutcho Copper, N. Murray Edwards, the
Keevil family, Bill Bennett, or any of the named BC/federal ministers (Spencer Chandra
Herbert, Murray Rankin, Bruce Ralston, Jagrup Brar, Tamara Davidson, George Heyman,
Sharon Hartwell, Nathan Cullen, Rebecca Alty, Rebecca Chartrand, Ellis Ross), or Chad
Norman Day/Kerry Carlick (TCG officials). Nor was any documented funding flow found from
any of those companies/individuals to any of the four NGOs, or vice versa.

**This is reported as an explicit, genuine negative finding, consistent with the
investigation's quality bar of not filling gaps with inference.** The four organizations'
funding bases, where identified, trace to environmental/conservation philanthropic
foundations (Moore, Hewlett, Wilburforce, Campion, Wild Salmon Center, Sitka, EDF) and a
US fiscal-sponsorship/foundation-management ecosystem (New Venture Fund/Arabella
Advisors, Tides Center, MakeWay) — not to the mining sector or government sources mapped
elsewhere in this investigation. If a mining-company or government funding tie to any of
these four organizations exists, it was not surfaced by the searches completed in this
pass.
