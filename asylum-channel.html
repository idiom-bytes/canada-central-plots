The Asylum Channel

Ottawa says immigration is being cut and the population is shrinking. That is true of permanent residents, work permits and study permits — all now falling from their 2024–25 peaks. One stream is the exception: asylum. It has never stopped rising, it is the one category where Canada does publish country of origin, and the half-million claimants now in the country are the measure of a system that takes them in far faster than it resolves or removes them.

How the channel was built

The half-million claimants did not arrive by accident, and not by force — the land border to the south is the most heavily policed on earth. The growth came through the front door, by policy: a chain of discrete federal decisions, most made by regulation and ministerial instruction rather than by any vote in Parliament.
