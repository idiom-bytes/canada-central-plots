The Real Cost

A figure of “$5 billion” circulates online. It is not a single official number — and the honest, sourced figures don't need the exaggeration. Federal asylum costs are large, rising fast, and only partly counted: health spending alone has more than quadrupled since 2020 and is headed past $1.5 billion a year. This page uses only Parliamentary Budget Officer and IRCC numbers.
