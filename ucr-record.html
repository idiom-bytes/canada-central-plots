About this view

Source: Statistics Canada, Uniform Crime Reporting Survey, Table 35-10-0177 (actual police-reported incidents, Canada). Read alongside the news-coverage Exploitation & Trafficking Record: that view counts cases that received media coverage (a proxy for attention); this view counts incidents recorded by police (the official measure). No legal definition of these offences changed in 2016–2024; StatCan notes increases partly reflect greater reporting and detection, not occurrence alone.
