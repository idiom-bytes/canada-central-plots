Narrative vs. Reality · Enforcement

Running to Stand Still

A fair correction to a common talking point: Canada is not deporting no one. CBSA removals hit a record ~23,000 in 2025 — the most in over a decade. The problem is arithmetic: removals are outpaced by new removable cases and a backlog that keeps doubling. Even record enforcement is losing ground, because the pressure is upstream — in intake, not at the exit.
