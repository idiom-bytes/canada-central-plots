Who Religious Hate Crime Actually Targets

Public and institutional attention to religious bias in Canada centres heavily on Islamophobia. The police-reported data tells a different story: in 2024, Jewish Canadians — about 1% of the population — were the target of 70% of all religion-motivated hate crime, at a rate more than twenty times that for Muslim Canadians. This page sizes the gap directly from Statistics Canada, and is transparent about the one number the viral version gets wrong.

What the official numbers show

The point is not that anti-Muslim hate is absent — it rose too (monthly incidents up ~68% versus the 2021–2023 baseline). It is that the measured burden, and especially the per-capita rate, falls overwhelmingly on Jewish Canadians, while public framing runs the other way. That gap between attention and data is the story — the same pattern this project tracks across immigration and asylum.
