Permanent vs. Temporary

Canada admits two very different streams of people: permanent immigrants, selected and tracked, and a far larger and faster-growing population of temporary residents — foreign workers, international students, and asylum claimants. This view sizes each, directly from Statistics Canada. No estimates, no search — official tables only.

What the official numbers show

Reading the data: the annual chart shows flows (net change in a year); the by-type stock chart shows the level (how many are present at a point in time). The net change by type chart bridges them — it splits each year's net flow into the same categories, and every bar totals the red "net" bar above (fiscal-year July-to-July deltas of the stock reconcile exactly to the official annual flow). So the +781k of 2023/24 is the same number, now shown as where it came from: work permits (+400k), study (+82k), asylum (+142k), work+study (+102k) and other (+54k). In 2024/25 the net turned slightly negative as study and work+study permits fell faster than asylum and work permits rose. Asylum figures count claimants, protected persons and related groups, whether or not they also hold a permit.
