Canada Is Letting Itself Burn › Chapter 3

The Carbon Nobody Counts

Source: Environment and Climate Change Canada, National Inventory Report 1990–2023 · Copernicus CAMS · Byrne et al., Nature 2024 · Canadian National Fire Database

Canada's official 2023 greenhouse gas total was 694 megatonnes of CO2 — the number reported to the UN and measured against Canada's climate targets. It does not include a single tonne of CO2 from wildfires. Canada reports wildfire emissions separately, under a UN provision for natural disturbances, and doesn't count them nationally. Independent estimates put the CO2 from 2023's wildfire season alone at 1.76 to 3 billion tonnes — two-and-a-half to over three times Canada's entire annual economy, in one fire season, unaccounted for. This chapter lays out the official numbers, the wildfire numbers nobody counts, and two honestly-labeled scenarios for what closing that gap could be worth.
