High — IP Ownership
TELUS Retains IP from $250M Public Investment
Despite $250M+ in public funds channelled through Canada Health Infoway to build PrescribeIT on TELUS Health's proprietary platform, TELUS retains ownership of the core transmission hub technology. The government cannot continue or redeploy the service without TELUS's consent. Parliament has not received the IP ownership agreements despite formal requests.

High — Audit Gap
No Auditor General Review Since 2009 / 2011
The 2009 OAG report found retroactive contracting, misleading adoption figures, and inadequate funding agreements. Despite $2.65B+ more in spending since then — including the entire PrescribeIT program — no public OAG performance audit of Infoway has been published since 2009/2011. PrescribeIT ran its entire lifecycle without independent performance auditing.

High — Pattern of Failure
Infoway Missed All 8 Program Targets in 2011 Audit
A 2011 external audit commissioned by Infoway's own board found it missed self-established targets in all eight major program areas. The 2009 OAG found Infoway misrepresented physician adoption rates (claiming 38% to get $500M; actual was 22%). PrescribeIT's <5% adoption repeats this pattern of overpromised, underdelivered outcomes.

Medium — Governance Structure
Arm's-Length Structure as Accountability Shield
Infoway's status as an independent not-for-profit allows Health Canada to claim it doesn't control Infoway's operations. Parliament cannot compel Infoway directly. The $250M TELUS contract was an Infoway-managed contract — outside Treasury Board procurement rules and Access to Information requirements. This structural gap has allowed $2.65B+ in spending to avoid meaningful parliamentary review.
HESA Meeting 25 — Committee Motion
Medium — Metric Integrity
Enrollment vs. Usage: Misleading Adoption Reporting
Infoway reported 16,185 prescribers and 7,953 pharmacies "enrolled" while actual prescription usage remained below 5%. This echoes the 2009 OAG finding where "available" was used to mean something far short of "used." The 2025 fee introduction (despite CPhA survey showing 65% of pharmacists would quit) suggests Infoway leadership was out of touch with provider realities.
PrescribeIT 2023 Year in Review
2001
Canada Health Infoway established. $500M federal seed funding from First Ministers' Action Plan. Total funding over 2001–2010: $2.1 billion.
Nov 2009
Auditor General Sheila Fraser: Infoway missed all 8 program targets. Retroactive contracting (13 of 35 contracts signed after work began). Misleading adoption stats: claimed 38% physician EMR access; actual was 22%.
2011
External audit commissioned by Infoway's own board (Bell Browne Molnar): Infoway missed self-established targets in all 8 major program areas. Only 16% of $2.168B earmarked for clinician adoption.
Budget 2016
$50M allocated to Infoway for e-prescribing and tele-homecare. PrescribeIT program begins. RFP (Call for Proposals) issued in July 2016.
Sep 2016
⚠ Red flag: Charles Harnick (former Ontario AG) registers as consultant lobbyist for TELUS Health, targeting Health Canada and Government Procurement — same month as PrescribeIT RFP evaluation.
Budget 2017
$300M allocated over 5 years for e-prescribing, virtual care, EMR. Total Infoway investment reaches $2.45B cumulative.
May 11, 2017
Canada Health Infoway selects TELUS Health as PrescribeIT technical provider. Platform built on proprietary TELUS Health Exchange.
Aug 2017
First electronic prescription sent through PrescribeIT (Huntsville, Ontario). Trial begins in Alberta and Ontario communities.
Apr 2023
Conservative MP Eric Duncan's written question Q-1262 answered — requested funding totals by year and usage by province. Data not publicly debated in committee.
May 2024
Infoway issues RFEOI for private-sector takeover, managed by PricewaterhouseCoopers. Deadline for Letters of Intent: December 19, 2024. No viable partner found.
Dec 2024
CPhA formally opposes $0.20/prescription fee. Survey of 1,300+ pharmacists: 65% would quit PrescribeIT if fee implemented. CPhA escalates to Health Minister.
Jan 1, 2025
$0.20/prescription pharmacy fee implemented despite CPhA warnings. Accelerates adoption collapse.
Jun 4, 2025
TELUS Health lobbyists meet with PMO Policy Advisor Joshua Swift and Opposition Leader's Office Policy Director Aaron Wudrick.
Jun 19, 2025
⚠ Critical: TELUS Health in-house lobbyists meet with Jocelyne Voisin (Health Canada Senior ADM who also sits on Infoway's board) and two other Health Canada officials.
Feb 4, 2026
Bill S-5 (Connected Care for Canadians Act) introduced in Senate — mandates health IT interoperability; does not recreate a centralized platform.
Feb 12, 2026
Canada Health Infoway announces PrescribeIT shutdown effective May 29, 2026. $250M+, 9 years, <5% prescription adoption.
Feb 17, 2026
Stuart Barnable (TELUS Health consultant lobbyist, former Senate Chief of Staff) ends H+K Strategies registration — same month as shutdown announcement.
Mar 12, 2026
HESA: Conservative MP Matt Strauss moves document production motion for all PrescribeIT contracts, amendments, IP agreements, termination notice, and payment history. Deadline: April 17, 2026.
Mar 24, 2026
⚠ Accountability blocked: Liberal MP Sonia Sidhu moves amendment to delete PrescribeIT contract records. Bloc MP Blanchette-Joncas moves adjournment (passes 5-0). No vote on disclosure.
Apr 15, 2026
HESA Meeting 27 scheduled (Standing Order 106(4)) to revisit unresolved PrescribeIT document production order. Outcome pending.
May 1, 2026
National e-prescribing open standard to be published publicly by Infoway.
May 29, 2026
PrescribeIT ceases all operations at 11:59 PM EST. Controlled substance prescriptions revert to physical secure pads in Alberta. Doctors back to fax.
