Where Does the Money Go?
Doers vs. Overhead in Canada's Public Sector

Canada's public sector is growing — but is the hiring going to frontline workers who deliver value, or to administrative and support roles that expand overhead? Three sectors examined independently: education, healthcare, and public administration.

Two complementary views: (1) where the money goes — CIHI's National Health Expenditure Trends data traces spending on hospitals, physicians, and system administration since 1975 — and (2) who is being hired — StatCan's payroll survey tracks employment growth across healthcare sub-sectors since 2001. Sector 3 — Public Administration
How Fast Is the Bureaucracy Growing?
Government employment relative to the population it serves. Canada's total public administration workforce grew from 28.1 per 1,000 Canadians in 2001 to 31.7 in 2025 — a 13% increase in per-capita terms. Local government is the fastest-growing level, rising from 10.7 to 12.6 per 1,000. The federal public service surged post-COVID and has only partially retreated from its 9.6 per 1,000 peak in 2022–23.

Methodology & Data Notes

Each sector uses only its own sector-specific data sources — there is no cross-sector comparison.

  • Education: StatCan Table 37-10-0065-01 reports annual financial data from all K-12 public school boards, broken into teachers' salaries, administration, school facilities, transportation, and other operating costs. Coverage: 1973–2023.
  • Healthcare (spending): CIHI NHEX tracks public-sector health spending since 1975 across eight use-of-funds categories. "Administration" covers health system management overhead (provincial health ministries, health authority administration) — not within-hospital administrative staff.
  • Healthcare (employment): StatCan SEPH Table 14-10-0220-01 tracks monthly payroll employment by 4-digit NAICS. December values are used as annual proxies. Sub-sectors: physician offices [6211], hospitals [622], nursing and residential care [623], social assistance [624], total health and social assistance [62]. Values are actual employee counts.
  • Public Administration: SEPH NAICS 91 (total public administration), 911 (federal), 912 (provincial/territorial), 913 (local/municipal/regional). Per-capita = (employment / population) × 1,000. Population from StatCan Table 17-10-0005-01, annual estimates.
