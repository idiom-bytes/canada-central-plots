# Source links — official statistics on origin (census & crime)

A shareable list of primary, official sources. Two parts:
**A.** Peer democracies that publish crime by national origin / immigrant background, in
aggregate. **B.** Canada's census, which publishes detailed personal characteristics in
aggregate — while Canadian crime statistics record no origin variables.

---

## A. Crime statistics by origin — official sources abroad

### 🇩🇪 Germany — Federal Criminal Police Office (BKA)
*Polizeiliche Kriminalstatistik (PKS) 2024* — suspects by German/non-German, by nationality, and by residence status.
- Suspects-by-nationality table set (view): https://www.bka.de/DE/AktuelleInformationen/StatistikenLagebilder/PolizeilicheKriminalstatistik/PKS2024/PKSTabellen/BundTVNationalitaet/bundTVNationalitaet.html
- Full PKS 2024 yearbook (PDF): https://www.polizei.hamburg/resource/blob/1053710/30efad000cc60586a22280031dad1ea0/pks-2024-jahrbuch-do-data.pdf

### 🇩🇰 Denmark — Ministry of Justice (research unit) & Statistics Denmark
*Kriminalitet og herkomst 2023* ("Crime and Origin") — convictions indexed by origin, age-standardised.
- Crime and Origin 2023 (PDF): https://www.justitsministeriet.dk/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Kriminalitet-og-herkomst-2023-WT.pdf
- Statistics Denmark, *Indvandrere i Danmark 2023* (publication, crime chapter): https://www.dst.dk/da/Statistik/nyheder-analyser-publ/Publikationer/VisPub?cid=47883

### 🇳🇴 Norway — Statistics Norway (SSB)
*Crime and punishment among immigrants and the remaining Norwegian population* — charge/sanction rates by immigrant group.
- Article (overview): https://www.ssb.no/en/sosiale-forhold-og-kriminalitet/artikler-og-publikasjoner/crime-and-punishment-among-immigrants-and-the-remaining-norwegian-population--328011
- Discussion paper (English, PDF): https://www.ssb.no/en/forskning/discussion-papers/_attachment/124152?_ts=13f5601b580

### 🇳🇱 Netherlands — Statistics Netherlands (CBS)
Registered crime by migration background (with controls for background characteristics).
- Report (PDF): https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-523f11e0-6364-4f20-ab95-ed083dd07532/pdf
- *Integratie en samenleven 2024* — crime chapter (web): https://longreads.cbs.nl/integratie-en-samenleven-2024/criminaliteit/

### 🇸🇪 Sweden — Brå (National Council for Crime Prevention)
*Registered offending among persons with domestic and foreign background* (2021:9), suspects 2007–2018, with age/sex/income controls.
- Report page (incl. English summary & table appendices): https://bra.se/rapporter/arkiv/2021-08-25-misstankta-for-brott-bland-personer-med-inrikes-respektive-utrikes-bakgrund
- English summary (PDF): https://bra.se/download/18.45e4b8e192705389a34c2b/1729515966490/2021_9_Registered_offending_among_persons.pdf
- Main report, Swedish (PDF): https://bra.se/download/18.45e4b8e192705389a34b52/1729514247826/2021_9_Misstankta_for_brott_bland_personer_med_inrikes_respektive_utrikes_bakgrund.pdf

## B. Canada's census — personal characteristics published in aggregate

### The questionnaire (the actual questions asked)
- 2021 Census long-form (Form 2A-L) — includes religion (Q30), ethnic/cultural origin, Indigenous identity, place of birth: https://www.statcan.gc.ca/en/statistical-programs/instrument/3901_Q2_V6
- 2021 Census questionnaires index: https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/ref/questionnaire/index-eng.cfm

### Reference guides (how each variable is collected & released)
- Religion: https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/ref/98-500/016/98-500-x2021016-eng.cfm  · PDF: https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/ref/98-500/016/98-500-x2021016-eng.pdf
- Ethnic or cultural origin: https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/ref/98-500/008/98-500-x2021008-eng.cfm  · PDF: https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/ref/98-500/008/98-500-x2021008-eng.pdf
- Visible minority & population group: https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/ref/98-500/006/98-500-x2021006-eng.cfm  · PDF: https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/ref/98-500/006/98-500-x2021006-eng.pdf

### Official release & published data tables
- The Daily — religious & ethnocultural diversity (2022-10-26): https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/221026/dq221026b-eng.htm
- Religion by visible minority and generation status (98-10-0342-01): https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=9810034201
- Visible minority by place of birth and generation status (98-10-0326-01): https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=9810032601
- Visible minority by ethnic or cultural origin (98-10-0337-01): https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=9810033701

> CSV downloads for the tables above: replace the page with
> `https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/tbl/csv/<8-digit-id>-eng.zip`
> (e.g. `98100342-eng.zip`), or use the "Download options" button on each table page.
