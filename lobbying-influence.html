Who Lobbies Ottawa on Immigration

Influence over immigration policy can be measured without speculation: every registered lobbying contact with a federal official is public record. This view aggregates a decade of those filings on the subject of immigration — who lobbies, how often, and which institutions they target.

The finding: economic interests drive the lobbying

What this is — and isn't: this measures registered lobbying activity, the formal, legal channel of influence. It does not capture informal influence, and a high contact count is not wrongdoing. It simply shows whose voices are loudest in the room when immigration policy is set.
